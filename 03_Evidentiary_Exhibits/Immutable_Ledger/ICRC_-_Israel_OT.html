Israel and the occupied territories

We have been present in Israel and the occupied territories since 1967 and have offices in Tel Aviv, Gaza, and across the West Bank, including East Jerusalem. We carry out our work in coordination with our partners, the Palestine Red Crescent Society and Magen David Adom in Israel.

Our work in Israel and the occupied territories

The ICRC has been present in Israel and the occupied territories (including the Golan, West Bank and Gaza Strip, and Shebaa Farms) since 1967.

As a neutral, impartial and independent humanitarian organization, we promote compliance with international humanitarian law (IHL) and work to mitigate the impact of armed conflict including occupation. Our protection activities include documentation of harm to civilians due to hostilities, working to find answers for those whose loved ones are unaccounted for, and visits to detainees.

In Gaza, our response is focused on lifesaving action and helping civilians affected by years of conflict . Our programmes enable communities to access medical care, economic and livelihood support, and raise awareness on unexploded ordinance. We also support continued delivery of water and wastewater services, dignified management of the dead, together with local organizations and authorities.

In the West Bank, we support communities with livelihood programmes, work to improve access to health care and water services through close coordination with the authorities. We engage with armed forces on the ground to facilitate the passage of ambulances.

In Israel and the occupied Golan, we support local organizations and authorities to help them to address needs identified by communities themselves, focusing on mental health and psychosocial support, resilience centres, and provision of medical equipment in communities far from major urban centres.

As a neutral intermediary, the ICRC facilitated the return of 195 people – including 35 deceased – held hostage in Gaza between October 2023 and December 2025; we continue working with authorities and communities in support of those affected. We also facilitated the return of thousands of detainees to Gaza under various ceasefire agreements, as well as hundreds of deceased people.

Urgent help needed

GAZA: MAKE A DONATION AND GIVE YOUR HELP

In Gaza, families continue to face the catastrophic humanitarian consequences of conflict. Your donation can help families affected by armed conflict meet urgent needs and recover with dignity. As a neutral, impartial and independent organization, the ICRC works to reach people affected by the conflict on all sides, helping provide medical care, essential household items, food, clean water and support for people separated from their loved ones. Your contribution helps the ICRC respond where needs are greatest.

Stories from the people we serve and our staff

During the latest escalation, the paramedics applied what they’d learned from the ICRC specialists, which helped them save many lives.

Dr Raafat Jaarour Head of the Emergency Medical Specialists Training and Capacity Development Department in Gaza

I went to the Artificial Limb and Polio Centre to get a prosthetic leg. They told me that the ICRC could help me start a business. The business is going well. I’ve hired a young man who, like me, lost his leg due to injury, and another young man with a physical disability.

Amro Coffee kiosk owner in Gaza

When the ICRC first visited me, I had started this small project. When I received the first round of support, I had greenhouses and wanted to set them up and expand my project. The idea grew bigger, and the greenhouses turned into bigger ones, stretching over two dunums of land.

Aysar Farmer in the West Bank

Our work with National Societies

The ICRC works closely with the Palestine Red Crescent Society in the West Bank, including East Jerusalem and the Gaza Strip, and with Magen David Adom in Israel. This includes coordinating humanitarian activities, exchanging technical knowledge, and providing support to respond to urgent needs.
